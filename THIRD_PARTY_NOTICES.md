## What this project uses from CCP

**Nothing, directly.** Unlike its sibling Imperial apps, this one has no
SDE dependency and no ESI/EVE SSO dependency at all - it's a local media
player, themed around EVE, that never contacts CCP's servers or reads
CCP's data files.

## Audio content - the real legal boundary for this app

This app **never extracts, scrapes, or derives audio from the installed
EVE Online client**. CCP's Third Party Policies explicitly ban this
("cache scraping," EULA §9.C), and it is actively enforced. This app's
music library only ever contains: (a) AI-generated original music, not
sampled or extracted from anything CCP owns; and/or (b) audio files the
user has personally, legitimately obtained (e.g. purchased from CCP's
own official Bandcamp store) and pointed the app at directly - the same
legal posture as any general-purpose media player pointed at a music
library the user already owns.

No CCP-owned artwork, logos, or other trademarked visual assets are
bundled with, embedded in, or committed to this repository. This app's
visual design is built entirely from scratch, not copied from official
CCP materials.
